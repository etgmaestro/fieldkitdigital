Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026 · Plain English stub for digital goods

Field Kit Digital (“we”) sells digital downloads at fieldkitdigital.com. This page explains what we collect and why. It is not legal advice.

What we collect

  • Purchase data: When the store is live, checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy (or a similar payment provider). They process your name, email, payment details, and order history under their own privacy policy.
  • Support email: If you write to support@fieldkitdigital.com, we keep the message content and your email so we can reply.
  • Site analytics: This static site may later add privacy-respecting analytics (e.g. page views without selling personal profiles). None are required to browse today.

What we don’t do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not need an account on this marketing site to browse products.
  • We do not collect payment card numbers ourselves — the payment provider does.

Downloads

After purchase, files are delivered digitally (download link and/or email from the store). Keep your own copy; re-download windows follow the store’s rules.

Cookies

The static marketing pages do not set tracking cookies by default. The checkout provider may set cookies on their domain when you buy.

Contact

Privacy questions: support@fieldkitdigital.com.
